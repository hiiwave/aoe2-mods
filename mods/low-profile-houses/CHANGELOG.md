# Changelog

## 2611.2-beta — 2026-11-17

- Fixed empty-space click selection in 108 graphics files, so shortened houses are easier to click around.
- Visuals, building footprints, collision and gameplay remain unchanged.
- Known limitation: hovering may still highlight a house above its visible roof.

Sampled in-game checks covered Western Feudal, Chinese Castle, Malian Feudal and Persian Castle houses, including damaged examples. Tested empty space and transparent corners did not select the house; visible roofs remained selectable. This is sampled coverage, not a test of every pixel or all civilizations.

## 2611.1-beta.5

- Earlier 0.6x beta with a 120 px upper bound and selected house variants.
- Publication-description formatting revision; not a new geometry treatment.
